About DB Entertainment

We are DB Entertainment - makers of your wedding memorable and unforgettable! Based in Ahmedabad, we travel all over India to bring joy and celebration to your special occasions.

With more than 20 years of experience in the wedding and event industry, our team consists of all professional and energetic artists who are passionate about creating magical moments. ✨😊

From traditional ceremonies to modern celebrations, we specialize in Safa & Paghdi, Panjabi Dhol performances, Entertainment Games for Haldi & Carnival, and Baraat on Wheels. Our dedication and expertise ensure that every event we handle becomes a cherished memory for you and your guests.
